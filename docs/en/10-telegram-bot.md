# Telegram Bot

## Getting Started

1. Find the bot in Telegram (ask the administrator for the bot name).
2. Tap the **"Start"** button or send the `/start` command.
3. The bot will automatically register you.

> **[Insert screenshot: Telegram screen with Start button]**

## Main Menu

After starting, the main menu appears with buttons:
- 🛍 **Products** — browse the catalog
- 🛒 **My cart** — view your cart
- 📦 **My orders** — view your orders
- 📰 **News** — read news
- 👤 **My profile** — personal information
- 📞 **Contact** — store contact information

> **[Insert screenshot: Bot main menu with all buttons]**

## Browsing Products

1. Tap the **"🛍 Products"** button.
2. A list of categories is displayed.
3. Select a category.
4. Products are shown with price and photo.
5. Use the **"◀️"** and **"▶️"** buttons for the next page.

> **[Insert screenshot: Product list in Telegram bot]**

## Adding to Cart

1. Under a product, tap the **"Add to cart"** button.
2. Select the quantity.
3. The product is added to your cart.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Add to cart" button under product]**

## Placing an Order

1. Tap the **"🛒 My cart"** button.
2. Review the items in your cart.
3. Tap the **"Place order"** button.
4. Send your delivery address as a message.
5. The order will be confirmed.

> **[Insert screenshot: Cart in Telegram bot with order button]**

## Tracking Orders

1. Tap the **"📦 My orders"** button.
2. A list of all orders with statuses is displayed.

> **[Insert screenshot: Order list in Telegram bot]**

## Bot Commands

Available commands:
- `/start` — start the bot
- `/help` — list of commands
- `/menu` — show main menu

## Admin Features in the Bot

If you have the **admin** or **manager** role, additional buttons are available:
- 📊 **Report** — sales report
- 📋 **Orders** — manage all orders
- ✏️ **Change status** — change order status
