# Cashier (Point of Sale)

## Accessing the Cashier

The cashier page is available to users with **seller**, **manager**, and **admin** roles.

1. Log in to the system.
2. Tap the **"Cashier"** button in the bottom navigation bar.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Cashier" button in bottom bar]**

## Cashier Interface

The cashier page consists of two parts:
- **Left side** — product catalog for quick selection
- **Right side** — sale cart with selected items

> **[Insert screenshot: Cashier page with catalog and cart]**

## Adding a Product to the Sale

1. Tap a product in the catalog on the left.
2. The product is added to the sale cart.
3. Change the quantity using the **"+"** and **"−"** buttons.

> **[Insert screenshot: Product added to sale cart]**

## Quick Product Search

1. In the search field, type the product name.
2. The product appears instantly.
3. Tap to add it to the cart.

> **[Insert screenshot: Quick search on cashier page]**

## Completing a Sale

1. Add products to the cart.
2. Verify the total price.
3. Tap the **"Sale"** button.
4. Select the payment method (cash / card).
5. Tap **"Confirm"**.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Sale" button with total price and payment selection]**

## Printing a Receipt

1. After completing the sale, tap the **"Print"** button.
2. The receipt will be prepared for printing.

> **[Insert screenshot: Sample receipt for printing]**

## Sales History

1. Tap the **"History"** button.
2. A list of all today's sales is displayed.

> **[Insert screenshot: Sales history with transaction list]**
