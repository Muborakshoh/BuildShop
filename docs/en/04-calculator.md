# Construction Materials Calculator

## Why Use the Calculator?

The calculator helps you estimate the amount of materials needed for construction or renovation. For example:
- How many bricks are needed for a wall?
- How many bags of cement are needed for a foundation?
- How many square meters of tiles are needed for a yard?

## Using the Calculator

1. Tap the **"Calculator"** button in the bottom navigation bar.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Calculator" button in bottom bar]**

2. Select the material type (e.g., bricks, cement, tiles).

> **[Insert screenshot: List of material types]**

3. Enter the dimensions:
   - **Length** — in meters
   - **Height** — in meters
   - **Width** — in meters (if required)

> **[Insert screenshot: Dimension input fields]**

4. Tap the **"Calculate"** button.

5. The result is displayed:
   - Required amount of material
   - Cost based on store prices

> **[Insert screenshot: Calculator result with quantity and price]**

## Adding the Result to Cart

1. After the calculation, tap the **"Add to cart"** button.
2. The calculated materials will be added to your cart.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Add to cart" button in calculator results]**
