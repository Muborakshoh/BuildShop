# Registration & Login

## Creating a New Account

1. Open the main page and tap the **"Login"** button in the bottom navigation bar.

> **[Insert screenshot: Bottom navigation bar with "Login" button]**

2. On the login page, switch to the **"Register"** tab.

> **[Insert screenshot: Login page with "Login" and "Register" tabs]**

3. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Full name** — your first and last name
   - **Email** — your email address
   - **Password** — your password (minimum 6 characters)

> **[Insert screenshot: Registration form with filled fields]**

4. Tap the **"Register"** button.

5. If email verification is enabled, enter the confirmation code sent to your email.

> **[Insert screenshot: Confirmation code input field]**

6. After successful registration, you will be automatically logged in.

## Logging In

1. Open the **"Login"** page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap the **"Login"** button.

> **[Insert screenshot: Login form with email and password fields]**

If you forgot your password, contact the store administrator.

## Logging Out

To log out, tap the **"Logout"** button in the top bar menu.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Logout" button in the user menu]**
