# Catalog & Products

## Browsing Categories

1. Tap the **"Products"** button in the bottom navigation bar.
2. The category list appears on the left side.
3. To view products in a specific category, tap its name.
4. To view all products, tap the **"All"** button.

## Searching Products

1. At the top of the catalog page there is a search bar.
2. Type the product name — results appear instantly.

## Product Card

Each product is displayed as a separate card:
- **Photo** — product image
- **Name** — product title
- **Price** — cost in somoni (TJS)
- **Availability** — "In stock" or "Out of stock"
- **"Add to cart"** button — to add the item to your cart

> **[Insert screenshot: Product card with price and "Add to cart" button]**

## Product Page

Tap a product card for full details:

- Large image
- Full description
- Price and unit of measurement
- Stock quantity
- Quantity selector and add-to-cart button
- Reviews and ratings

> **[Insert screenshot: Full product page with image, price, quantity, and reviews]**

## Writing a Review

1. Navigate to the product page.
2. In the **"Reviews"** section, tap the stars (1-5).
3. Write your review text.
4. Tap **"Submit"**.
