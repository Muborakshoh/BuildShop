# Cart & Orders

## Adding to Cart

1. On the catalog page or product page, tap the **"Add to cart"** button.
2. Select the quantity.
3. The item will be added to your cart.

> **[Insert screenshot: "Add to cart" button on product card]**

## Viewing the Cart

1. Tap the **"Cart"** button in the bottom navigation bar.
2. The list of selected products is displayed.

> **[Insert screenshot: Cart page with product list]**

## Changing Quantity

1. In the cart, use the **"+"** and **"−"** buttons to change the quantity.
2. The total price is recalculated automatically.

> **[Insert screenshot: Cart with quantity buttons and total price]**

## Removing an Item from Cart

1. Tap the **"Remove"** button (✕) next to the product.
2. The item will be removed from the cart.

> **[Insert screenshot: Remove button next to item]**

## Placing an Order

1. Add items to your cart.
2. Tap the **"Place order"** button.
3. Enter the delivery address.
4. Select a payment method.
5. Tap **"Confirm"**.

> **[Insert screenshot: Order form with address and payment fields]**

## Tracking Your Order

1. Go to the **"Orders"** page.
2. A list of all your orders is displayed.
3. Order statuses:
   - **New** — order received
   - **Confirmed** — order confirmed
   - **In transit** — product shipped
   - **Delivered** — product delivered to you
   - **Cancelled** — order cancelled

> **[Insert screenshot: Order list with different statuses]**

## Viewing Order Details

1. Tap an order in the list.
2. Full information is displayed:
   - Product list
   - Price and quantity of each item
   - Total cost
   - Order status
   - Order date

> **[Insert screenshot: Order details page]**
