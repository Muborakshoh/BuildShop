# Product Management

## Product List

1. In the admin panel, go to the **"Products"** section.
2. A list of all products is displayed with:
   - Image
   - Name
   - Category
   - Price
   - Stock quantity

> **[Insert screenshot: Product list in admin panel]**

## Adding a New Product

1. Tap the **"New product"** button.
2. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Name** — in three languages (Tajik, Russian, English)
   - **Description** — in three languages
   - **Category** — select from the list
   - **Price** — product price in somoni
   - **Unit of measurement** — piece, kg, meter, etc.
   - **Image** — upload a product photo

> **[Insert screenshot: New product form with all fields]**

3. Tap **"Save"**.

## Editing a Product

1. In the product list, tap the **"Edit"** button (✏️).
2. Modify the necessary fields.
3. Tap **"Save"**.

> **[Insert screenshot: Product edit form]**

## Deleting a Product

1. In the product list, tap the **"Delete"** button (🗑️).
2. Confirm the deletion.

> **[Insert screenshot: Delete button with confirmation dialog]**

## Inventory Management

1. On the product page, go to the **"Inventory"** section.
2. Enter the product quantity.
3. Tap **"Update"**.

> **[Insert screenshot: Inventory management section with quantity field]**

## Search and Filter

1. At the top of the list, use the search bar.
2. Select a category to filter by.
3. Results appear instantly.
